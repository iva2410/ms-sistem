MODELI
======
U ovaj folder idu istrenirani nnU-Net modeli sa Google Drive-a. Za svaki model
preuzmi ceo folder skupa (dovoljni su plans.json, dataset.json i fold_0/checkpoint_final.pth):

  Master/mozak/modeli/nnUNet_results/Dataset001_MsBrain    -> modeli/Dataset001_MsBrain
  Master/mozak/modeli/nnUNet_results/Dataset021_BrainNew   -> modeli/Dataset021_BrainNew
  Master/kicma/modeli/nnUNet_results/Dataset013_SpineNOREG -> modeli/Dataset013_SpineNOREG

Primer strukture:

  modeli/Dataset001_MsBrain/nnUNetTrainer_100epochs__nnUNetPlans__3d_fullres/
      plans.json
      dataset.json
      fold_0/checkpoint_final.pth

Folder "validation" i fajlovi checkpoint_best/checkpoint_latest nisu potrebni.
Stranica "Podesavanja" u aplikaciji pokazuje koji modeli su pronadjeni.
